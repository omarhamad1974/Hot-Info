# Phase 12c: Animation

Upload this folder to GitHub (replace the files), wait 1-2 minutes, then open admin.html > Website content > **Animation**.

## The whole site
- **Animation when a section or card appears**: the default for every section.
  Standard: fade, fade up, fade down, slide from the left or right, zoom in, zoom out.
  Advanced: blur to sharp, flip up (3D), rotate in, wipe up from the bottom. "No animation" is also a choice.
- **When the mouse is over a card**: the look the site has now, no movement, lift, grow, colored glow,
  accent border, or zoom the picture inside the card (works on cards that have a picture, such as news).
- **More options**: speed (seconds), wait before it starts, distance it travels, movement style
  (smooth, soft, slow start and end, snappy, little bounce, even speed) and "play again every time it scrolls into view".
- **Turn off all animations on the website**: one tick box.
- Press **Play** to see a sample of the settings.

## Each section
Open a section in the list. Anything left on "Same as ..." follows the whole site.
- **Section animation**: the headings and blocks of that section.
- **Cards appear with**: a different animation just for the cards (for example fade for the section, flip for the cards).
- **Cards come one after the other**: 0 to 0.5 seconds between one card and the next.
- **Mouse over a card** and the same **More options**.
A section that has its own settings shows a "Custom" badge.

Press **Save to GitHub**. Visitors whose device asks for less motion never see the animations.
Leaving everything on its default keeps the website exactly as it is today.

# Phase 12b: Sections and Contact

Upload this folder to GitHub (replace the files), wait 1-2 minutes, then open admin.html > Website content.

## Sections tab (was "Backgrounds")
- **Width of all sections**: two sliders, one for tablet and one for desktop.
- Open any section in the list:
  - **Hide this section on the website**: the section and its menu links disappear. Untick to show it again.
  - **Width on tablet / desktop**: "Same as the whole site" or 50% to 100% for this section only.
  - **Background**: Default, a color (your brand palette is listed first) or a photo with darkening, as before.
- The list now also contains "Home page: Picture carousel". The three "contact box" blocks on the service pages
  can be hidden or given a background, but have no width of their own (they sit inside another section).
- A grey "Hidden" badge shows which sections are off. Press Save to GitHub.

## Contact tab
- **WhatsApp business number**: digits with the country code, no + or spaces (example 201006552255).
  It is used by the WhatsApp box at the bottom of every page and by the "WhatsApp us" buttons.
- **Contact details**: any number of phones (optional label), the email and the address.
  The About page shows all of them; the service pages show the email and the first phone.
  Arabic address: switch the admin page to عربي > General.
- **Map**: latitude and longitude for the About page map. Empty = map hidden.
- **Social media**: LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, Telegram. Only filled boxes show an icon,
  in the header of every page. (Addresses must start with https://.)

# Phase 13a: Menu and HOT News

Upload this folder to GitHub (replace the files), wait 1-2 minutes.

1. The section and menu name **Jurisdictions** is now **HOT News** (Arabic: أخبار HOT). It changes by itself.
   To use another wording, edit the names in the Menu tab (menu items) or in the Arabic text overrides (UI texts).
2. admin.html > Website content > **Menu** > press **Customise the menu**.
3. For each item you can change the name in English and in Arabic, where it opens, the icon, and whether it appears
   in the top menu (big screens), the bottom bar, both, or is hidden. Use the arrows to reorder, "Add an item" for more.
4. Press **Save to GitHub**. All pages now use this one menu. 6 items or fewer look best in the bottom bar.
5. "Go back to the built-in menus" removes your menu and restores the old behaviour.
